# `= this.file.name `
---

Thrives in full sun, well-drained soil, and a warm environment. Water when the soil feels dry to the touch.

- **Sunlight:** Oregano needs full sun (6-8 hours daily) for strong flavor and growth. In hotter climates, partial shade in the afternoon can be beneficial. 

- **Soil:** Well-drained, lean to average soil is ideal. It doesn't need much fertilization. 

- **Temperature:** Oregano prefers temperatures between 60 and 80 degrees Fahrenheit. 

- **Watering:** Water when the soil feels dry to the touch, allowing it to dry out between waterings. 

- **Planting:** Plant in spring after the last frost, or start indoors 6-10 weeks before that. 

- **Spacing:** Space plants 8-10 inches apart. 

- **Harvesting:** Harvest leaves by snipping or pinching branches just above a leaf node, ensuring you don't cut back more than one-third of the plant at a time. Regular harvesting encourages bushier growth.
