#### Ingredients
---
- 1 1/2 lbs (2-3 ) chicken breаsts cut in half for 4 cutlets
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt or more to taste
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic herb mix
- 2 large eggs beaten until frothy
- 1/2 cup Panko bread crumbs
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese shredded
- 2 Tbsp extra light olive oil to sauté or more as needed 
- 24 oz marinara sauce
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese 

#### Marinara Ingredients
---
- 2 Tbsp olive oil  
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 medium yellow onion diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 28 oz crushed tomatoes (or blended diced tomatoes)
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt 
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 Tbsp basil or parsley, finely minced (optional, add 2-3 min before completion)
- 1 carrot (optional)

#### Instructions
---
- Preheat oven to 400
- Cut chicken breasts into thin halves, 4 - 6 cutlets ideally 
- Dry cutlets and season with salt, pepper and garlic herb mix, set aside
- Complete **Marinara Sauce Instructions** before continuing 
- Prepare 3 separate dredging bowls, fully submerge and shake cutlets in each
	Bowl
	1. Flour 
	2. Beaten eggs
	3. Panko and parmesan mixed
- In a large pan, fry the cutlets over medium-low heat with olive oil 4-5 min (or until brown)
- Flip cutlets once achieved desired brownness and continue cooking until nearly cooked through, 155 - 165 degrees internal
- Place chicken on a large drying rack
- Once marinara sauce is complete, spread a thin layer on a deep baking dish and spread chicken on top
- Add a dollop of marinara sauce on each chicken top and spread mozzarella cheese over the dish
- Bake for 15 min and top with parsley
- Serve with spaghetti noodles,  garlic toast or mac and cheese

#### Marinara Sauce Instructions
---
- Fry onions (and carrots) in a medium sauce pan over medium heat with oil 1 - 2 min
- Add garlic and cook until aromatic 1 - 2 min
- Add tomato paste and stir frequently 2 - 3 min
- Add tomatoes, oregano, salt and pepper
- Increase heat and bring to a boil
- Lower temperature and let simmer for 30 - 45 min, stirring occasionally. Continue chicken instructions while simmering
