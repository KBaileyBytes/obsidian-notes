### **Ingredients**

- 1 large **English cucumber** (or 2-3 Persian cucumbers)
- 1/2 teaspoon **salt** (for drawing out moisture)
- 1 tablespoon **rice vinegar** (or white vinegar)
- 1 teaspoon **soy sauce** (adds umami)
- 1 teaspoon **sesame oil** (for a nutty depth)
- 1 teaspoon **honey** (to balance acidity)
- 1/2 teaspoon **red pepper flakes** (optional, for heat)
- 1 teaspoon **sesame seeds** (for crunch)
- 1 clove **garlic, minced**
- 1 green onion, thinly sliced (optional, for garnish)

---
### **Instructions**

1. **Prep the Cucumbers**
    - Slice the cucumber **thinly** (about 1/8 inch thick). If using an English cucumber, you can leave the skin on. If using regular cucumbers, peeling is recommended.
    - Place slices in a colander, sprinkle with **salt**, and toss. Let them sit for **10-15 minutes** to draw out excess water.
    - After resting, **rinse** the cucumber slices and pat them dry with a paper towel.
2. **Make the Dressing**
    - In a small bowl, whisk together **rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, honey, red pepper flakes, garlic, and sesame seeds**.
3. **Combine & Chill**
    - Toss the cucumber slices with the dressing.
    - Let it sit for **at least 10 minutes** (or up to an hour in the fridge) to absorb the flavors.
4. **Serve**
    - Garnish with **green onions** and extra sesame seeds before serving.

---
### **Tips & Variations**
- **Add carrots** for extra crunch and color.
- **Use chili oil** instead of red pepper flakes for extra heat.
- **Swap honey for sugar** if you prefer a different sweetener.
- **Make it creamier** by adding a little Greek yogurt or mayo.
