#### Cherry Tomatoes

Provide them with ample sunlight (6-8 hours), well-drained soil, consistent moisture, and support for their vines

1. Watering and Fertilizing:
	- **Watering:** Keep the soil consistently moist but not soggy. 
	- **Deep watering:** Water deeply and less frequently to encourage deep root development. 
	- **Fertilizing:** Use a tomato-specific fertilizer during the growing season.

2. Support and Pruning:
- **Support:**
    Indeterminate varieties (most cherry tomatoes) need support, such as stakes or cages. 
- **Pruning:**
    Regularly remove suckers (side shoots) from the main stem to encourage fruit production. 

3. Harvesting: 
	- **Harvest regularly:** Pick ripe cherry tomatoes as soon as they are ripe to encourage more fruit production.

4. Container Gardening: 
	- **Containers:** Choose large, well-draining containers for tomato plants.
	- **Container soil:** Use a rich, well-drained potting mix.

#### Basil

Choose a sunny location (6-8 hours of sunlight daily), use rich, well-drained soil, and water deeply when the top inch is dry. Pinch off flower buds to encourage foliage growth, and harvest leaves by pinching them off where they meet the stem, or by snipping stems, to promote continuous leaf production.

- **Location:**
    Choose a sunny spot with at least **6-8** hours of direct sunlight daily. In hotter climates, consider providing afternoon shade. 

1. Watering and Care: 
- **Watering:** Water basil deeply when the top inch of soil is dry. Basil thrives in moist soil, but avoid overwatering.
- **Mulching:** Apply a 3-inch layer of mulch around the plants to help retain soil moisture and suppress weeds.
- **Fertilizing:** Feed basil plants regularly with a water-soluble plant food.

1. Pinching and Harvesting:
- **Pinching:**
    As basil plants grow to 4-6 inches tall, pinch off the topmost branches to encourage more side shoots and bushy growth. 
- **Harvesting:**
    Start harvesting leaves when the plant is 6-8 inches tall. Pinch off leaves where they meet the stem, or snip stems to harvest larger amounts. 
- **Flower Bud Removal:**
    Pinch off flower buds to prevent the plant from going to seed and to promote more leaf production.

#### Thyme

Thyme thrives in a warm, sunny location with well-drained soil. Avoid overwatering and prune after flowering to encourage bushy growth

1. **Watering:** Water only when the soil is dry, allowing it to dry completely between waterings.
2. **Harvesting:** You can harvest fresh thyme leaves at any time of the year.
3. **Pruning:** Trim plants back after flowering to encourage bushy growth.

#### Leaf Lettuce
Choose a sunny location (or one with partial shade in hot climates), ensure well-drained soil, and provide consistent moisture. Harvest leaves as needed, starting with the outermost leaves when they are large enough to eat.

**1.** **Location:**
	Lettuce thrives in full sun but can tolerate partial shade, especially in warmer climates. 

**2.** **Soil and Watering:**
    Ensure the soil is well-drained and enriched with compost. 
    **Avoid water stress:** Lack of water can cause lettuce to taste bitter. 
    Water consistently, keeping the soil moist but not soggy, especially during hot weather. 

**3.** **Harvest:**
    Harvest leaf lettuce leaves as needed, starting with the outermost leaves once they are large enough to eat. You can harvest leaves from the same plant throughout the growing season. 

#### Parsley

Choose a location with full sun to partial shade, use well-drained soil enriched with organic matter, and keep the soil consistently moist.

**1.** **Care**:
- **Watering:** Keep the soil consistently moist, but avoid overwatering. 
- **Sunlight:** Parsley prefers full sun to partial shade. It needs at least six hours of sunlight a day.
- **Harvesting:** Cut leaves from the base of the plant to encourage new growth.

#### Rosemary

Provide it with a sunny location, well-draining soil, and **minimal** watering, especially once established. Rosemary thrives in full sun and prefers soil that doesn't stay wet.

**1.** **Location:**
- Choose a sunny spot that receives at least 6 hours of sunlight daily, or preferably more. 
- **Soil:** Rosemary prefers well-draining soil. If you have heavy clay soil, amend it with grit or pumice, use raised beds, or grow in containers. 
- **Spacing:** Space plants 2-3 feet apart, especially if you're in a warmer climate where rosemary can grow large. 

**2.** **Watering:**

**Drought Tolerance:**
    Rosemary is relatively drought-tolerant, but new plants need regular watering for at least the first summer. 
**Established Plants:**
    Once established, water only when the soil is dry to the touch, especially in containers.
**Container Plants:**
    Water container plants more frequently than those in the ground, as they dry out faster. 

**3.** **Light:**
- **Full Sun:** Rosemary thrives in full sun.
- **Partial Shade:** It can tolerate partial shade, but growth will be less.
- **Indoor Growing:** If growing indoors, provide supplemental light if necessary. 

**4.** **Harvest:**
- **Regular Pruning:**
    Prune rosemary regularly to encourage bushier growth and prevent it from becoming woody. 
- **Annual Prune:**
    Perform a heavier prune annually in spring or early summer after the flowers finish.
