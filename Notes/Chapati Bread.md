- 6 cups all purpose flour (895 grams)
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 4 cups lukewarm water
- cooking oil
<br>

#### Instructions 
---
- Whisk first 3 ingredients together in a large mixing bowl
- add 3 cups lukewarm water and mix into large dough, save 1 and add as needed
- once together, let sit for 5 - 10 min
- prepare a large bowl of flour for dusting and coat your surface heavily
- stretch and knead dough into large ball, adding flour as needed
	- part way through, knead dough into coin shape and pour 1 tbsp oil on top and stretch and knead inwards, back to ball shape
- let sit for 5 - 10 min covered on kitchen surface
- cut into golf balls, roughly 15 total
- stretch dough balls into long ovals roughly 1 cm thick and 12 " long with rolling pin
- coat top of stretched dough with cooking oil (or butter or ghee)
- dust oiled top with flour
- coat dough in flour and roll length-wise into long tubes, then inwards into a cinnamon roll shape
- set formed rolled on baking sheet
- preheat pan on med high heat
- starting with the first rested chapati, re-coat with flour and roll into tortilla shape for each one
- set plate, oil, spatula, parchment paper and kitchen towel aside
- clear pan of any oil
- fry each side 30 seconds turning frequently and spraying with oil
- will turn bubbly and brown once complete
- set aside under towel 
- serve with soup, stew, coffee, eggs, chili, tacos etc
