#### Ingredients
- 1 lb 4 oz chicken breast cut into 1 inch slices
- 1 head broccoli 
- 1 tsp salt + pepper
- 2 tsp garlic + ginger minced
- 3 small oranges
- 1/4 cup corn starch
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1 tbsb vinegar
- 1/4 cup sugar (white or brown)
- 1 tsp red chili flakes
- (Optional) 1 tbsp honey
- (Optional) 1 green onion
- Roasted sesame seeds for garnish

#### Instructions

(Optional)
Marinate the chicken slices a few hours previously with the following:
- 1 tsp of salt + pepper + garlic mix
- 1 orange worth of juice
- 1 tsp corn starch

1. Boil the broccoli over high heat for 5 min or until fork - tender, add water as necessary 
2. Plunge broccoli in ice bath and set aside
3. Coat chicken in baking pan with 1/4 cup corn starch or until no skin showing
4. Fry the chicken with a heavy amount of cooking oil over medium - high heat for 6 - 7 minutes or until golden brown
5. Give the chicken a toss and stir in garlic and ginger
6. Stir in soy sauce, vinegar and 2 oranges worth of juice
7. Add sugar and broccoli from the ice bath, the water from the broccoli will help liquidate the sauce
8. Shave 1 full orange over the dish, allow to sit for a few minutes simmering to thicken the sauce
9. Add corn starch (thicken) or water (thin) to achieve desired viscosity
10. Cook for 4 - 5 min, finish with red chili flakes
11. Serve with rice, garnish with roasted sesame seeds and green onion
