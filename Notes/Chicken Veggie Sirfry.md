### Ingredients
- 3 servings of spaghetti noodles
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 lb 8 oz chicken breast cut into bites
- 1 cup corn
- 1 carrot julienned 
- 1 bell pepper cubed
- 2 cup snap peas
- 1 thumb of ginger grated
- 1 tsp garlic minced
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 cup pasta water
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes

*Optional*
- Green Onions 
	*- dice thinly and cook white part with garlic + ginger, save green for garnish*
- Roasted sesame seeds for garnish

#### Instructions

If chicken is raw:
- Cut breasts into 1 inch cubes / bites
- Season with salt and black pepper
- Fry over medium heat with 1 tsp sesame seed oil, stirring frequently
- Once cooked, set aside the cooked chicken and continue instructions

 With cooked chicken:
 - Cook spaghetti noodles with 1 tsp of sea salt
 - Once al dente, drain the noodles and save a small portion of pasta water for later (at least 2 cup)
 - Fry garlic and ginger over medium heat for 2 min or until fragrant
 - Add carrots, bell pepper, and snap peas stirring frequently for 3 - 4 min
 - Add corn, chicken, and sesame seed oil and cook for an additional 2 - 3 min
 - Add noodles to pan and top with honey, soy sauce, and 1 cup of pasta water
 - Once dish is thoroughly mixed, cook for an additional 2 - 3 min, adding extra pasta water if required 
 - Top with red pepper
 - Serve and garnish with roasted sesame seeds and green onion
