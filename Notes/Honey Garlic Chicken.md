### Ingredients 
- 1 lb  10 oz chicken breast , boneless and skinless (3 pieces cut in half)
- Salt and pepper
- 1/4 cup flour
- 3 1/2 tbsp (50g) unsalted butter (or 2 1/2 tbsp olive oil)
- 4 garlic cloves , minced
- 1 1/2 tbsp apple cider vinegar (or white or other clear vinegar)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce , light or all purpose
- 1/3 cup honey (or maple syrup)

**Optional**
-  Green onion
-  Ginger

### Instructions
- Cut the breasts in half horizontally to create 6 steaks in total. Sprinkle each side with salt and pepper.
- **Optional** -
	  Marinate and coat the chicken a few hours before cooking with:
	  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
	  - 1 tbsp olive oil
	  - 1 tsp garlic powder (or fresh)
	  - 1/2 tsp black pepper
	  - 1 tbsp corn starch
- Place flour in a shallow dish. Coat chicken in flour and shake off excess.
- Melt most of the butter in a large skillet over high heat – hold back about 1 tsp for later.
- Place chicken in skillet and cook for 2 – 3 minutes until golden. Turn and cook the other side for 1 - 2 minute. Continue flipping until each is cooked through and place on a separate plate
- Turn heat down slightly to medium low.
- Make a bit of room in the pan and add garlic (and green onion and ginger) and top with remaining dab of butter. Cook 1 - 2 min or until aromatic.
- Add vinegar, soy sauce and honey. Stir / shake pan to combine. Bring sauce to simmer and add back the chicken, then simmer for 1 minute or until slightly thickened.
- Turn chicken to coat in sauce. If the sauce gets too thick, add a touch of water and stir. If the sauce is too thin, slowly stir in water + corn starch slurry until desired thickness.
- Remove from stove immediately. Place chicken on plates and drizzle over remaining sauce.
