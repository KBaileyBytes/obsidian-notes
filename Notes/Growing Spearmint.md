1. **Soil and Location**:

	- **Sunlight:** Spearmint prefers partial shade, but it can tolerate full sun. 
	- **Soil:** Choose well-draining soil that is moist but not waterlogged. 
	- **Moisture:** Keep the soil consistently moist, but avoid overwatering, as this can lead to root rot. 

2. **Care**:

	- **Fertilization:** Mint doesn't require much fertilizer, but you can fertilize with a balanced liquid fertilizer in early spring and every 6-8 weeks during the growing season if grown in containers. 
	- **Watering:** Water regularly, especially during dry spells, ensuring the soil doesn't dry out completely. 
	- **Pruning:** Prune spearmint regularly to promote new growth and prevent it from becoming woody. 
	- **Division:** Divide spearmint plants every 2-3 years to maintain their health. 
	- **Harvesting:** Harvest leaves as needed throughout the growing season, before they bloom.
