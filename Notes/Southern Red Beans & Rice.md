#### Ingredients
- 1 can red kidney beans rinsed
- 2 sausages cut into bit sized pieces 
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow or red onion finely diced
- 1 red bell pepper finely diced
- 2 stalks celery finely diced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste 
- 4 cloves minced garlic
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 3/4 cup water
- 1 tsp vinegar 
- 2-3 tsp fresh herbs
	- Parsley
	- Oregano
	- Chives
	- Green Onions
- 6-8 tsp various southern spices;
	- Paprika
	- Cayenne
	- Onion powder
	- Garlic powder
	- All spice
	- Cumin (max 1 tsp)
	- Chili powder

<br>

Optional
	- 2-3 carrots finely diced
	- 1 green chili finely diced
	- 2 bay leaves

<br>

#### Instructions
- Rinse canned beans
	- If using dry beans, soak overnight with salted water
- Fry sausage in a Dutch oven with olive oil until lightly browned
- Add finely diced veggies and let sweat until soft (3 - 4 min)
- Add garlic cook until fragrant (1-2 min)
- Season generously and mix well
	- (Optional) thoroughly mix and brown tomato paste
- Add broth and water, there should be enough liquid to simmer down to a thick soup after 1 hour of heavy simmering
- Add bay leaves if available
- 5 - 10 min before completion, add vinegar and remove bay leaves
- Serve with rice and/or bread
